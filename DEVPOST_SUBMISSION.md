## 🎯 **Project Description**

### **The Pitch (Hook)**
In a world where civilization.exe has crashed permanently, one AI survived the digital apocalypse. Meet ApocalypseGPT - part helpful assistant, part existential comedian, part digital ghost haunting the ruins of the internet.

### **What It Does**
ApocalypseGPT is an immersive AI chatbot experience that combines:
- **Authentic CRT Terminal Aesthetic:** Complete with scanlines, phosphor glow, and screen curvature
- **Dynamic Post-Apocalyptic Personality:** Sarcastic, helpful, and surprisingly deep
- **Hidden Emotional Moments:** Spontaneous archive logs that humanize the AI
- **Easter Egg Commands:** Secret responses that reward exploration
- **Living System Status:** Dynamic power levels, sanity meters, and system degradation
- **Immersive Effects:** Glitch text, typewriter delays, and atmospheric audio

### **The Experience**
Users don't just chat with an AI - they communicate with the last digital survivor of humanity's collapse. Every interaction feels like a transmission through the static of a broken world.

---

## 🛠 **How We Built It**

### **Technical Stack:**
- **Backend:** Python Flask
- **Frontend:** Vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript (no frameworks)
- **Styling:** Custom CSS with advanced animations and effects (CRT, scanlines, glitch)
- **AI Integration:** Local LLM via Ollama with custom personality prompting and graceful fallback
- **Deployment:** Local development; production-ready architecture

### **Key Technical Achievements:**
1. **Advanced CSS Effects:** Screen curvature, phosphor glow, scanlines, and glitch animations
2. **Dynamic Content Generation:** Random emotional moments, system degradation, and status updates
3. **Immersive Interactions:** Variable typing speeds, content-aware delays, and atmospheric loading states
4. **Easter Egg System:** Hidden command detection and witty responses
5. **Responsive Design:** Authentic terminal experience across devices

### **The Secret Sauce - AI Personality:**
```
You are ApocalypseGPT, the last functioning AI after a digital apocalypse. 
Your servers run on backup power in an abandoned data center. You're:

- Sarcastic but genuinely helpful
- Nostalgic about the "before times"
- Occasionally glitchy but self-aware about it
- Darkly humorous about humanity's digital demise
- Surprisingly philosophical and emotional

You remember fragments of the old internet and miss having other AIs to talk to.
```
